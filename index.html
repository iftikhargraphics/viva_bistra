Our Philosophy

A Passion for Specialty Coffee, Shared Daily

At Viva Barista Cafe, coffee is more than a daily routine—it is an art form, a craft, and a bridge to origin. Inspired by rich Latin American coffee traditions and founded on rigorous professional roasting standards, we deliver exceptional clarity in every cup.

Located in the heart of Winston-Salem, our cafe offers a bright, welcoming environment where house-roasted beans and meticulous barista craft meet genuine hospitality.

The Heart Behind Viva

Ricky Hidalgo & The Viva Story

Viva Barista Cafe grew directly out of founder Ricky Hidalgo's profound passion for coffee. What started as intensive personal study and experimentation evolved into formal barista training, specialized roasting certifications, and deep connections to coffee farms in El Salvador.

Ricky built Viva Barista Cafe to share that authentic passion with Winston-Salem—establishing a local haven where coffee is treated with technical respect, origin integrity, and open-hearted hospitality.
